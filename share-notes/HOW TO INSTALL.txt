CLASS PACK - a mod for Worst Class Ever

HOW TO INSTALL

1. Close Worst Class Ever if it is open.
2. In Steam, right-click Worst Class Ever, then Manage, then Browse local files.
3. Open the "Mods" folder you see there.
4. Copy the whole "classpack" folder from this zip into that Mods folder.
5. Start the game and open the mod menu.
6. Switch "Class Pack" on, then restart the game.

Everyone in the room should have the same version of the mod.
The extra stuff only works in rooms where the room owner has the mod too.

WHAT IT ADDS

- Phone with DoorDash: as a student, press O during class to pull up your phone.
  Open the DoorDash app and order. The food is dropped in the teacher's office.
  Go and get it, then eat it for 15 mischief.
- Prank call: the phone also has a Call app. Ring the teacher. If the teacher does not
  press H to answer in time, the teacher loses stamina.
- Nerf guns: a rare find. Each time a student finishes a snack there is a small chance
  a gun turns up in their desk drawer, and the teacher gets a warning. Press B in your
  seat to grab it, or open the drawer, look at the gun floating over it and press the
  same key you pick up snacks with. A teacher who eats a snack from a student's drawer
  can find one too, more rarely. Every gun has one shot. Press X, or click the mouse wheel, to
  shoot. A student who is hit goes up on a pole and can't be rescued. A teacher who is
  hit falls over. The RPG is the rarest and hits everybody, you too.
- Fire alarm: a red box hangs above a classroom door. A student presses B under it,
  once per class. While it rings, a teacher who runs slips over.
- Window poles: a few extra poles stand by the classroom windows for hanging students.
- Hungry teacher: the teacher stands next to a desk and presses G to eat a snack
  from its drawer and get stamina back.
- Power cut: once per class the lights go out for a bit.
- Play alone: the room owner can start a class with only the bot classmates.
- Owner settings: the room owner presses F6 between classes to set class length,
  number of teachers and points to win.

TO REMOVE IT

Switch it off in the mod menu, or delete the "classpack" folder from the Mods folder.
