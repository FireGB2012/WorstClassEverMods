CLASS PACK - a mod for Worst Class Ever

HOW TO INSTALL

1. Close Worst Class Ever if it is open.
2. In Steam, right-click Worst Class Ever, then Manage, then Browse local files.
3. Open the "Mods" folder you see there.
4. Copy the whole "classpack" folder from this zip into that Mods folder.
   Updating? Replace the old "classpack" folder with the new one.
5. Start the game and open the mod menu.
6. Switch "Class Pack" on, then restart the game.

Everyone in the room should have the same version of the mod.
The extra stuff only works in rooms where the room owner has the mod too.
In class, press F1 to see what you can do right now.

WHAT IT ADDS

New classes
- Swim class: a pool hall with bots that swim. In the water, hold P to pee in the pool
  for mischief (double next to the teacher), press B to splash the teacher's screen,
  and crawl (Ctrl) to dive under. The pick-up key (E) climbs the pool ladders and the
  diving board's steps.
- Dance class: a disco hall where everybody has to dance. Q does the class dance,
  J does the worm: mischief every few seconds while you keep it up, but you only creep
  along, so the teacher can catch you at it.
- Elevator panel: beside the blackboard. Between classes, anyone looks at a button and
  presses E or B to pick the next class: the pool, the dance hall or the classroom.

Phones
- Phones are things you find. Each time you finish a snack there is a 7.4% chance that
  a phone is in your desk drawer. Grab it with E, or with B from your seat.
- In the swim and dance halls (no desks) a phone turns up now and then on the coffee
  table in the teachers' lounge or on the snack table. The first one to grab it gets it.
- In class, O pulls your phone up. Everybody can see you on it and the teacher hears
  it scribbling. One use, then it is gone:
    DoorDash: order food. It is dropped in the teacher's office (in the swim class on
      the lounge table, in the dance class on the snack table). Go and get it, then
      eat it for mischief.
    Prank call: ring the teacher. If the teacher does not press H to answer in time,
      the teacher loses stamina.
    Tricks: paper airplane, water balloon, super glue, laser pointer, whoopee cushion,
      pass a note or take a nap. The trick goes in your hand: X (or the mouse wheel)
      uses it, B puts it away.

Wardrobe: O, in the lobby or in the break (not during class)
- Hats, sunglasses, a backpack, a cape or wings. Hair: the game's own, an afro, dreads,
  a mohawk or spiky hair, in any colour. Face sliders (eyes, pupils, head size) and a
  thin-to-fat body slider. Recolour clothes and skin, or go bald.
- Everybody with the mod sees your look, and it stays from game to game.
- The bots dress like the players.

New teachers: pick one in the wardrobe's Teacher tab
- Gym teacher: X (or the right mouse button) throws a pizza slice that knocks a
  student over.
- Lunch lady: X throws mashed potatoes that stick a student in place. She can't write
  on the blackboard: standing next to her pot fills up her stamina.
- Janitor: X mops. That clears banana peels, glue and whoopee cushions around him and
  leaves a wet floor that students slip on.
- Bald with a wig: a student who sneaks up behind him presses B to yank the wig off.
  No chalk until he walks over and gets it back.
- In the swim and dance halls the teacher's stamina drains at half speed and fills up
  fast at the lifeguard chair or the DJ booth.

Nerf guns
- A rare find in your desk drawer after a snack (the teacher gets a warning). Every gun
  has one shot: X or the mouse wheel. Whoever is hit falls over like a rag doll for
  4 seconds, and meanwhile the teacher can walk up and grab them. Students can shoot the
  teacher too: the teacher is out for 5 seconds. The RPG is the rarest and hits everybody.

More
- Window hanging: a student tied to a pole by a window hangs outside it. From inside the
  room you only see their fingers on the sill.
- Fire alarm: a red box hangs above a classroom door. A student presses B under it, once
  per class. While it rings, a teacher who runs slips over.
- Hungry teacher: the teacher stands next to a desk and presses G to eat a snack from its
  drawer and get stamina back.
- Power cut: once per class the lights go out for a bit.
- The "Caught 0/1" count at the top of the screen is hidden.
- Play alone: the room owner can start a class with only the bot classmates.
- Owner settings: the room owner presses F6 between classes for the class length, the
  number of teachers, the points to win, the next class and more.

TO REMOVE IT

Switch it off in the mod menu, or delete the "classpack" folder from the Mods folder.
